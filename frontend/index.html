    About this system

    Every answer is assembled from records retrieved from the graph: NVD for CVE entries, and MITRE for CWE, CAPEC and ATT&CK. Relationships between records are taken from the cross-references published in those feeds and are never inferred. When the evidence does not support an answer the assistant says so instead of guessing. The benchmark figures shown with answers come from an offline evaluation set and are not a guarantee for an individual answer.
